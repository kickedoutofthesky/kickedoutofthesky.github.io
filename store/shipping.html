Pricing

All prices are displayed in USD. If you are outside the United States, your credit card issuer or payment processor may convert the amount to your local currency. Stripe's hosted checkout may also display the total in your local currency, but the transaction will still be processed and settled in USD.

Import Duties & VAT

Prices in USD. Orders outside the US may be subject to import duties or VAT charged on delivery.

When ordering from countries outside the United States, you may be responsible for paying additional import duties or Value Added Tax (VAT) assessed on the package upon delivery. These charges are determined by the destination country's customs and tax regulations and are not included in the price displayed on our site or the amount charged to your card.

Common destination countries apply different tax rules:

  • VAT countries (EU, UK, etc.): VAT will be included in the price on checkout.
  • Tax countries (US, Canada, etc.): Sales tax or GST may apply at checkout.
  • Duty-free countries: Some countries do not assess duties on goods under a certain value.
  • Other regions: Import duties may apply upon delivery and must be paid by the recipient.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number from Printful. You can use this number to monitor your package's journey in real time via your carrier's website (typically UPS, DHL, or a regional carrier).
