ABOUT THE SONG

“Wasting My Life Away” is an introspective anthem about the battle for sobriety — the feeling of being stuck in a loop, fighting to find meaning while the world keeps spinning. Driven by soaring guitars, raw emotion, and an infectious chorus, it captures the modern pop-punk revival’s heart while delivering a deeply personal message.

ABOUT THE ARTIST

Kicked Out of the Sky is the solo project of Matt Bachman (guitar, vocals). Formed in Los Angeles in 2020 and now based in Lisbon, the project blends emotional storytelling with high-energy pop-punk and indie rock influences. Matt’s music channels the golden era of 2000s punk while embracing cinematic modern production.
