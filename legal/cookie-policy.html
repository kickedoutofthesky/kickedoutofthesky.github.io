Cookie Policy

Last updated:

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, provide a better user experience, and give site owners information about how the site is used.

2. How We Use Cookies

kickedoutofthesky.com uses a minimal number of cookies. We do not use advertising or targeting cookies. The cookies we use fall into two categories: essential and analytics.

3. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They include:

Cookie Purpose Duration
Stripe session cookies Required to process secure payments through Stripe during checkout Session
localStorage (cart data) Stores your shopping cart items and selected product filters locally in your browser Persistent until cleared

4. Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website. These cookies collect information in an anonymized form.

Cookie Purpose Duration
_ga Distinguishes unique users by assigning a randomly generated number as a client identifier 2 years
_ga_* Used by GA4 to persist session state 2 years
_gid Distinguishes users for analytics purposes 24 hours

5. Advertising Cookies

We do not use any advertising, marketing, or tracking cookies. We do not serve targeted ads, and we do not participate in any ad networks.

6. Third-Party Cookies

The following third-party services may set cookies when you use our site:

  • Google Analytics — Analytics cookies as described above. See Google's Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — Payment processing cookies necessary for secure checkout. See Stripe's Privacy Policy

7. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Here is how to manage cookies in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data

8. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, some parts of the site may not function correctly:

  • Shopping cart functionality relies on localStorage (not technically a cookie, but similar browser storage)
  • The checkout process requires Stripe session cookies to process payments securely
  • Disabling analytics cookies will not affect site functionality

9. Cookie Consent

By continuing to use kickedoutofthesky.com, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or clearing cookies.

10. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
