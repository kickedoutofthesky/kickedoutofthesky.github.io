Accessibility Statement

Last updated:

1. Our Commitment

Kicked Out of the Sky and Let Go Media are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

2. Standards

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

3. Current Accessibility Features

We have taken the following steps to make kickedoutofthesky.com more accessible:

  • Semantic HTML: We use proper heading hierarchy and semantic elements to help screen readers navigate content
  • Alt text: Images include descriptive alternative text
  • Keyboard navigation: Interactive elements can be accessed using keyboard navigation
  • Color contrast: We aim for sufficient contrast ratios between text and background colors
  • Responsive design: The website is designed to work on devices of various sizes and orientations
  • Focus indicators: Interactive elements have visible focus styles for keyboard users
  • Form labels: Form fields include associated labels for screen reader users
  • Skip navigation: We provide mechanisms to skip repetitive navigation content where possible

4. Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some areas of the site may not be fully accessible. Known limitations include:

  • Some older content may not meet all WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards
  • Third-party content and integrations (Stripe checkout, embedded media) may have their own accessibility limitations outside our direct control
  • Some decorative images or design elements may not have alternative text where they are purely presentational
  • Audio content (music) does not include transcripts or captions — lyrics are provided where available

5. Accessible Alternatives

If you encounter accessibility barriers on any page, please contact us. We will work to provide the information you need in an accessible format. For example:

  • We can provide product descriptions in plain text via email
  • We can assist with placing orders if the online store is difficult to navigate
  • Lyrics and other text content can be provided in alternative formats upon request

6. Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of kickedoutofthesky.com. If you encounter accessibility barriers, find content that is difficult to use, or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us:

Email: christa@letgomedia.com
Subject line: "Accessibility Feedback"

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days and to resolve issues as quickly as possible.

7. Enforcement and Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may file a complaint with the relevant authorities. In the United States, this may include the U.S. Department of Justice, Civil Rights Division . In the European Union, you may contact your national enforcement body under the European Accessibility Act.

8. Continuous Improvement

We regularly review our site for accessibility issues and are committed to making ongoing improvements. This statement will be updated as we make progress toward full WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance.
