2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Cookies: we use essential and analytics cookies as described in our Cookie Policy

4. Purpose of Data Collection

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Order fulfillment: processing and delivering merchandise orders
  • Payment processing: securely processing payments through Stripe
  • Site analytics: understanding how visitors use our site to improve the experience
  • Customer communication: responding to inquiries, sending order updates and tracking information

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to fulfill your merchandise orders (name, address, payment)
  • Legitimate interest: site analytics to improve our website and services
  • Consent: marketing emails (if you opt in) and non-essential cookies

6. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share your personal data with the following third parties, solely for the purposes described:

  • Stripe (payment processing): receives your payment card data and billing address to process transactions securely. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Printful (order fulfillment): receives your name and shipping address to produce and ship merchandise. Printful Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (site analytics): collects anonymized usage data, IP addresses, and cookies. Google Privacy Policy
  • Vercel (hosting): hosts our backend API and serverless functions. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages (hosting): hosts the frontend static site

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Order data: retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes
  • Analytics data: retained according to Google Analytics default settings (14 months)
  • Customer inquiries: retained for up to 2 years after the last communication

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure: request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to restrict processing: request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to withdraw consent: withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint: file a complaint with your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at christa@letgomedia.com .

9. Your Rights Under CCPA

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know: request disclosure of the personal information we collect, use, and share
  • Right to delete: request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale: we do not sell your personal data

To exercise these rights, contact us at christa@letgomedia.com .
